This dashboard displays the validated input data from data/hepatocyte_features.pt and data/hepatocyte_baseline_data.json (MyGene.info v3, STRING-db v12.0, GTEx/HPA Healthy & NCBI GEO Steatosis/Cholestasis). Each cell below includes its own dedicated user guide.

📘 User Guide Cell 1: Causal Hepatocyte AOP Network Graph (Visualization 1)

  • How to use: Click the blue buttons below to switch between the 5 cellular states. Hover your mouse over a node (gene) or arrow (interaction) to inspect the exact TPM values, MyGene.info structural data, STRING-db scores, and the official OECD AOP Key Event citation.
  • What you see happening:
    • In Steatosis (AOP 34), the coral-red lipogenesis genes (NR1H3, SREBF1, FASN, ACACA, SCD, CD36) swell strongly (outlined in yellow: >+0.8 log2FC), while the turquoise homeostasis genes (PPARA, CPT1A, MTTP, APOB) shrink.
    • In Cholestasis (AOP 27), the bile salt export pump ABCB11 (BSEP) shrinks dramatically and CYP7A1 and SLC10A1 are repressed via FXR (NR1H4), while on the right the purple stress and apoptosis cascade (NFE2L2 → NFKB1 → TP53 → CASP3) lights up strongly.
    • In the two Δlog2FC modes, upregulated genes turn bright red and repressed genes turn blue, with node size scaling with the magnitude of perturbation.

📘 User Guide Cell 2: Healthy vs. Pathological Expression Profile (Visualization 2)

  • How to use: Compare per gene (on the x-axis) the three colored bars in Panel A (left) for absolute mRNA expression $\log_2(\text{TPM}+1)$, and inspect in Panel B (right) the relative shift ($\Delta\log_2\text{Fold-Change}$) above or below the zero line. Hover over any bar for the exact number.
  • What you see happening: This chart illustrates why the three attractors in Milestone 1.2 and Milestone 3 ($z_{\text{homeo}}$, $z_{\text{steatose}}$, $z_{\text{cholestase}}$) are sharply distinguishable: Steatosis (pink bars on the right) has an exclusive peak on genes 0 through 5, Cholestasis (yellow bars on the right) has a deep dip on genes 11 through 13, and both pathologies converge on the stress/apoptosis genes 14 through 17.

  • What you see happening: All three states are normalized relative to the same healthy baseline ($\mu_{\text{healthy}}, \sigma_{\text{healthy}}$). 📘 User Guide Cell 4: [19 × 19] Graphormer Shortest-Path Matrix D & Centrality Encoding (Visualization 4)

  • How to use: Inspect in Panel A (left) the shortest-path distance between every pair of nodes in the network (computed via Floyd-Warshall), and in Panel B (right) the number of incoming (blue) and outgoing (pink) AOP interactions per gene.
  • What you see happening: Row and column 00:[CLS] form the virtual Graphormer Readout node with special distance 10 (purple) to all 18 genes and 0 to itself. Within the 18 genes (indices 1 through 18), shortest paths range from 0 (diagonal, blue) and 1 (directly connected, green) up to 8 (red, e.g., from bile acid synthesis CYP7A1 all the way to lipogenic enzyme ACACA).
