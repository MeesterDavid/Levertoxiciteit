🧪 Milestone 0 (Step A Baseline): Microsoft Graphormer + ToxTransformer MIE Predictor

Model 1 (2D Molecular Graph): Microsoft Graphormer (clefourrier/pcqm4mv2_graphormer_base, 47.7M params, 12 layers, 768D on GPU 1)
Model 2 (SMILES → SELFIES & Bioassay MoE): ToxTransformer (biobricks-ai/toxtransformer, 29.3M params, 2 Transformer experts × 3,797 bioassays, 512D on GPU 1)

This program constitutes Step A (Dual Molecular Structure & SMILES Baseline) from ARCHITECTURE.md: it converts SMILES molecules into both 2D atom/bond graphs and SELFIES sequences and predicts per model and in consensus whether a compound triggers a Molecular Initiating Event (MIE) (MIE 167: LXRα activation → Steatosis or MIE 41: BSEP inhibition → Cholestasis).

📘 User Guide Cell 4: Summary Table (Graphormer & ToxTransformer) & Bridge to Milestones 1 through 4

  • Key conclusion for your research (Validation Strategy Step A → Step B → Step C): Even when combining two powerful molecular models (Microsoft Graphormer for 2D graph topology and ToxTransformer for SMILES/bioassay prediction), they only answer the static question: "Can this molecule bind to LXRα (MIE 167) or BSEP (MIE 41)?"
